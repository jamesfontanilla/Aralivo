# Privacy notice

Aralivo stores private learning progress, tasks, notes, cards, focus history, and preferences for the authenticated learner. Notes and detailed answers are not included in email, external AI context unless the learner explicitly enables note context, or learning receipts.

Learning receipts are opt-in and contain only a schema version, pseudonymous identifier, content identifier, achievement type, completion timestamp, issuer key, content version, hash, transaction metadata, and network. They are learning records, not degrees or official credentials.

Optional providers may be disabled. `.ics` export works without Google. Users can export data, clear local data, revoke integrations, and delete their account.
